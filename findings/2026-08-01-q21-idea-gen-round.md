robustly clear zero: maker-YES@bid mean +1.07¢, **block-bootstrap 95% CI [−2.42¢, +3.89¢] straddles zero**; maker-NO@bid
+4.24¢ but that is a **NO-base-rate artifact** (the wide population settles NO 62% of the time — buying NO cheap is
directional base-rate exposure, not spread capture). So S68's entire edge lives or dies on the unmodeled maker
fill-rate / adverse-selection question — precisely the wall (L5) that killed S6 (naive single-side income, modal-spread
fee-death), S13/S23 (single-side directional maker), and S14 (crypto-ladder overround, winner leg fills 93% and costs
